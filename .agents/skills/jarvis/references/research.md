# Research

## Sources

- Use official sources and vendor documentation first. Apply [verification.md:Sources](verification.md#sources) to evidence and unchecked claims.
- Use a third-party report to identify a question or supply evidence the original source does not publish. Record its author, date, method and relevant limits. Separate its inference from a checked fact.
- Check a third-party claim against the original source when available. State a conflict or missing source rather than presenting the report as official policy.
- Apply [decisions.md:Evidence and risk](decisions.md#evidence-and-risk) to each vendor's current terms and AI-use policy before using its service.
- For a long-form cited report, use the deep-research package when available. This reference adds no separate report procedure.

## Legal answers

- Give the original legal source and its checked date with each legal answer. State the jurisdiction and facts to which the answer applies.
- Record the event that requires another check, such as a changed rule, jurisdiction or relevant fact. Reuse follows [decisions.md:Evidence and risk](decisions.md#evidence-and-risk).
- Leave an answer unchecked when its required source cannot be read. Do not treat a third-party summary as the legal authority.

## Audits

- State the standard, scope and period before measuring work. Read evidence from its source and compare actual results with that standard.
- Measure cost, elapsed time, waits, repeated work and unresolved failures when those measures serve the stated audit. Record missing evidence and each gap's owner and closing check.
- For a scheduled audit, record its due time and scope in [long-session.md:Handoff](long-session.md#handoff). Compare with the prior audit, verify open actions and set their next check.
- For a documentation-growth audit, read changed and added guidance. Check whether each section has a current task, one rule owner and a reading route. Remove repeated or obsolete instructions through their source owner. Keep facts in the home defined by [memory.md:Home](memory.md#home).
- Report results through [communication.md:Message types](communication.md#message-types). Keep open audit actions in the handoff until their checks pass.
