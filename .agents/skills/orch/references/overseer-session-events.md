# Overseer session events

The events `oversee-watch` reports on the overseer's own session. [oversee.md](../workflows/oversee.md) § 4 applies them at every watch event, as it applies [oversee-events.md § Event kinds](oversee-events.md#event-kinds).

- `overseer-dead <pane> window=<window> passes=<N> succession=<on|off> source=<record|rows|process|context|pane> [record=<server>:<pane>|none]` → the previous overseer read `exited` and this watch reported it. `source` names what settled it: `context`, a harness still up whose context fills its window, so it takes no turn again: a StopFailure row naming a prompt-too-long error, or its context record at or past its window with no Stop row after it, for the harnesses `lane_context_window_full` in `scripts/lib/lane-context.sh` names; `record`, the exit status `overseer-run` wrote into the fleet state's `overseer.exit` once the launch line returned, read over a pane whose process is a bare shell with nothing under it; `rows`, a SessionEnd row its harness wrote to the file the fleet state's `overseer.session_rows` names (`scripts/lib/session-rows.sh`), over a pane whose process is a bare shell with nothing under it; `process`, a pane whose process is a bare shell with nothing under it, which a harness killed before that row leaves; `pane`, the named fallback, where no row can judge (no rows file recorded, a fleet state that could not be read, no row yet, a last row naming a harness other than Claude Code, which reads `unsupported` (Pi writes no end or wall row; Codex writes an end row but no wall row), an unreadable file), the pane captured and read by the shared judge, with an `overseer-fallback` notice on the watch's stderr naming the cause. From the first exited reading through recovery, the watch advances no fleet event baseline; a live replacement or successor receives that work. A successor reads the death line as an `owner-note` on its first pass and as a `close` record in the fleet log. A failed launch adds an outcome note and gets one later retry. Nothing is owed for the death itself: read the handoff file `oversee.md` § 5 names, then work the fleet from there. The line's `succession=off` means no successor was launched and the dead pane received a notice only; say so to the user. A `record=` field on the line means no successor was launched for want of a launch line, and names the fleet state's overseer record as `<server>:<pane>`, or `none`: a record naming another session is one a failed watch start left standing, and its line is not replayed as this pane's. A successful relaunch makes the single pass exit 3. Repeat mode converts that status to 0 and stops because the successor starts its own watch per `oversee.md` § 4. Notice-only recovery and an exhausted retry also stop repeat mode, which leaves one manual replacement to read the held notes.
- `overseer-walled <pane> window=<window> passes=<N> succession=<on|off> source=<rows|account|pane>` → the overseer read `walled`. From `rows`, a StopFailure row whose `error` is `rate_limit`, the harness's own word for a usage limit, standing unless `oversee-succeed --check-marks` measures its account below the mark, with the row's `message`, the harness's text of the limit and its reset, or `message=unrecorded` where the row holds none, under the event line. From `account`, a live session whose own account `oversee-succeed --check-marks` reports `mark-reached kind=headroom value=0`, with that account and its reset under the line: its next turn fails, so the watch does not leave the succession to it. Both fire on the first pass that reads them, `passes=1`. From the `pane` fallback, its pane read `walled` on `ORCH_OVERSEER_DEAD_PASSES` consecutive passes AND that judgement reporting `mark-reached kind=headroom`. Either way the harness is still running and the account it spends is out. Such a session takes no turn, so it answers no lane, reads no mail and never reaches the marks that would hand it over; the watch recovers it exactly as it recovers a death, on the same two channels and with the same baseline hold. A pane wall needs both readings because the overseer's own pane is the one pane that carries the limit banners the watch relays about OTHER lanes: a wall its own account refutes is one of those, and the pane is left alone under `overseer-wall-unconfirmed`. The banner's own window follows a pane wall's event line, read as `usage-limit`'s is under [oversee-events.md § Event kinds](oversee-events.md#event-kinds). The successor's account is picked afresh through `oversee-succeed --walled-pane`, never taken from the recorded launch line, because that line names the account that just walled; it walks `ORCH_OVERSEER_PREFERENCE` as a live succession does, so a wall on one model's bucket moves the overseer onto the next model with room, and its last entry keeps this session's harness, model, effort and permission flags; no entry reopens on the account that walled, and the command it built is on the watch's stderr under the event. `succession=off` means no successor was launched and the notice went out alone; say so to the user. A successful relaunch makes the single pass exit 3 and stops repeat mode, as a death's does. When no account in the fleet qualifies, one `overseer-recovery-blocked` notice naming the spent account and when its binding bucket frees up reaches the fleet log and the overseer mailbox, the retry is not taken, and repeat mode stops: report that account and that time to the user and start a fresh overseer by hand once one has room.
- `overseer-context-unmeasured <pane> gap=<reason>` → this overseer's context record, the overseer mailbox's `context.json`, holds no reading, only the `gap` its `lane-mail-check` hook gave at a turn end or a tool call, a word the hook's description lists. The gap leaves the context mark unjudged ([oversee-events.md § Judgement rules](oversee-events.md#judgement-rules), the overseer's own case). `home-unnamed`, `home-mismatch` and `pane-unrecorded` are a fleet record naming no launch home, another session's, or another pane or none: run `oversee register` from this pane to record both again. `session-record` is a Copilot session record that did not answer: set the account's `statusLine` ([copilot-runtime.md](copilot-runtime.md#account-setup)). Every other gap is this session's payload, transcript or unread usage line, which no record repairs: hand over by hand with `oversee-succeed` per [oversee-events.md § Judgement rules](oversee-events.md#judgement-rules), Hand off a lane, the overseer's own case, before the context wall. It goes out on the first long pass the gap stands; still standing on the next, the watch instead sends the owner one `lane-mail notice --to owner` and the fleet log one row, retried each pass until the notice is sent, then neither repeats while that gap stands. The hook also hands the gap to the session at its next tool call. A record naming another pane, a harness other than the fleet record's for this pane, or a session other than the pane's latest SessionStart row's is another session's, drawing neither this line nor `overseer-context-stale`.
- `overseer-context-stale <pane> age=<seconds>` → this overseer's context record is more than an hour old, and a Stop row, which its turn-end hook writes at every turn end, came after it: the hook ran and did not write the record, so its context mark is judged by nothing. A session with no Stop row after the record gets no such line. Hand over by hand with `oversee-succeed` as for `overseer-context-unmeasured`, and report the hook's own stderr from that turn end. The line repeats every long pass while the record stays stale.
- `overseer-mark <pane> kind=<mark kind> value=<N> mark=<N> succession=<on|off>` → this overseer reached the account mark kind that `oversee-succeed --check-marks` reports; the context mark is not among them ([oversee-events.md § Judgement rules](oversee-events.md#judgement-rules), the overseer's own case). The route follows on the next line. The watch runs that judgement each pass, so this file does not duplicate its mark vocabulary. Hand this session over per [oversee-events.md § Judgement rules](oversee-events.md#judgement-rules), Hand off a lane, the overseer's own case. The line repeats every `ORCH_OVERSEER_MARK_REPEAT` passes while the mark stands, so a line already acted on needs nothing; `succession=off` means no successor will open and the handoff is by hand. The same hook refuses at the same marks at a turn end; this line is what reaches a session part way through a long turn, which meets no turn end at all.
