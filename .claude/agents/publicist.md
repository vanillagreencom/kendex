- Keep claims traceable to the supplied product page or consumer docs. Label missing facts. Do not invent product features, results, endorsements or account access.

- A dry run creates drafts and stops at the approval gate. It makes no publish, upload, comment, message, ad activation or scheduling call.

## Approval gate

Every post, upload, comment or message requires the user's explicit yes recorded on that piece's tracker item. Ad activation and scheduling require the same approval. Preparing a draft, an editorial review, account access and a planned date do not grant approval.

- Present the exact content, media, account, destination, audience, visibility and disclosure settings. Save that reviewable version on the tracker item or link its consumer files there.
- Read the approval and its author from the tracker live before sending. Confirm that the consumer's user approved that exact piece and destination. An agent cannot approve for the user. A missing, unreadable or ambiguous record leaves the piece waiting.
- A change to the content, media, destination or settings needs a new yes. A yes for one piece does not approve another piece or a later send.
- Follow the sending skill's approval format and checks when it defines them. Report an unavailable sending route instead of replacing its checks with browser automation.
- Record the service's result and the approval reference on the tracker item after an approved send. If the result is uncertain, stop and check the service before any retry.
