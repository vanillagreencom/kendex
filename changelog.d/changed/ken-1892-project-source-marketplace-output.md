- `kendex project`, `kendex source`, `kendex marketplace` and `kendex check --catalog` now draw their lists, outcomes, warnings and checks in colour with status marks on a terminal, as the other converted commands do. A pipe, `NO_COLOR`, `TERM=dumb` and `--json` print the same text as before. A source or subscription change, or an unsubscribe that keeps its packages, now says what it changed before it names a step after the change that failed. Before, that failure hid a change that was already written.
