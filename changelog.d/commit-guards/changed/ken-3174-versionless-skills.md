- A `SKILL.md` that `COMMIT_GUARDS_CHANGELOG_PACKAGE_PATHS` selects and whose frontmatter holds no `version:` key line declares a versionless package: a change to it owes no version raise, and its entries collate under its bare name. A `version:` line outside `metadata:`, or an empty `metadata.version`, is a collection error rather than a versionless package. `install-git-hooks --check` judges a copy of the package under another skill root by its name and a checksum of its scripts, not by its version.
