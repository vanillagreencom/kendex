- Relative links in skills from a path catalog reach the same file once installed, at the shared tree and at every tool's copy, and keep reaching it after the skill is kept on unsubscribe, adopted, forked, imported into a catalog or saved and installed as a template. Links within a skill, to sibling skills or already reaching their target keep every byte. A link that cannot reach its target warns with `agent-link-unrepresentable` at install and `skill-link-kept` from the command that moved it, in the terminal and the desktop app.
