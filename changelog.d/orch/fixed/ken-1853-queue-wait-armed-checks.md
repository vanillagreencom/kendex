- `queue-wait` now names an armed PR that GitHub has not yet enqueued. While its required checks are still running, the verdict is `armed_awaiting_checks`, and merge-pr waits again on the same head under a `QUEUE_WAIT_ARMED_MINUTES` wall-clock budget (default 90) without spending a ci-fix recovery cycle. A failed check, or every check green with still no queue entry, is `armed_blocked`, which takes the recovery cycle. `progress_unobservable` now means only that a queue entry exists and its head could not be read.
