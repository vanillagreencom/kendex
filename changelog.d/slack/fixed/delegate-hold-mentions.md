- The Slack relay's Jarvis session hold no longer pages the owner for work the session already handles. When `SLACK_JARVIS_SESSION_FILE` moves to a file not yet written, a running relay keeps holding on the replaced file until the new one is fresh or the replaced one stale. At the end of a hold, an open ask at or below the session's `to-overseer.seen` count is journaled as skipped, with no post and no mention. `slack post --mention` posts with no mention while the hold stands and prints one `mention-held` line on stderr.
