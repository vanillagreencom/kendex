Bookmark a marketplace package or bundle

Usage: kendex bookmark add --kind <KIND> --source <SOURCE> <NAME>

Arguments:
  <NAME>
          

Options:
      --kind <KIND>
          What it is: a package kind, or `bundle`

      --source <SOURCE>
          The marketplace it comes from — `owner/repo`, a marketplace link, or a
          folder

  -h, --help
          Print help
