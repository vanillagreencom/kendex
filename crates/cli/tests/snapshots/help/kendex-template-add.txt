Add packages to a template

Usage: kendex template add [OPTIONS] <NAME>

Arguments:
  <NAME>
          

Options:
      --source <SOURCE>
          The marketplace these packages come from — `owner/repo` or a folder.
          Naming it also tells two members of one kind and name apart; without
          it a removal reaches every member wearing them

  -a, --agent <AGENT>
          

  -s, --skill <SKILL>
          

      --hook <HOOK>
          

      --command <COMMAND>
          

      --mcp-server <MCP_SERVER>
          

  -b, --bundle <BUNDLE>
          Bundles the marketplace offers, each installed whole

      --plugin <PLUGIN>
          Plugins, each installed whole like a bundle

      --from-project <FROM_PROJECT>
          Take a package's current files from this project, replacing the
          template's own copy of it

      --confirm-license
          Confirm the marketplace's license allows copying, where the files
          taken are a marketplace's and kendex recognizes its license

      --license-basis <LICENSE_BASIS>
          Your reason for copying, where kendex does not recognize the license
          those files came under

  -y, --yes
          

  -h, --help
          Print help
