# Trash

Covers: crates/core/src/trash.rs, crates/cli/src/commands/trash_cmd.rs

Where every removal's bytes go, and the bound that keeps a host from filling with them. Removal never deletes; the pass at the end of a writing verb is the other half of that promise.

## Boundaries

- `crates/core/src/trash.rs::move_to_trash` is the one writer: the apply engine's `Trash` op, the project restore and a Pi package's replacement or removal land through it, under a name that opens with the moment it was moved. A name without that stamp is not kendex's and is neither listed nor removed. Enforced by `crates/core/src/trash/tests.rs::a_name_is_dated_by_the_stamp_it_opens_with` and `::a_listing_reports_name_age_and_bytes_newest_first`.
- A completed `apply`, `refresh`, `remove` or `update-pi` calls the pass once, after its own writes and before its closing line, through `crates/cli/src/commands/engine_common.rs::tidy_trash`; a `remove` whose later scope fails still reaches it, an `apply` that stops before its last scope is written does not, and `updates --apply` reaches it as the `refresh` it hands off to (`crates/cli/src/commands/updates_cmd.rs::run`). Every other CLI verb that writes through the apply engine or `pi_ext`, a plan-only `apply` and the app call no pass: an entry they leave waits for the next of the four. The order and the plan-only case are enforced by `crates/cli/tests/trash_cli.rs::refresh_and_remove_close_on_the_pass_too`, `::an_apply_brings_the_trash_within_its_bounds_and_keeps_what_it_wrote`, `::a_plan_only_apply_leaves_the_trash_alone`, `::a_remove_that_fails_at_a_later_scope_still_closes_the_scopes_it_wrote` and `crates/cli/tests/update_pi.rs::update_reinstalls_from_the_declared_source`; that the other verbs and the app call no pass is not mechanically enforced.

## Invariants

1. The keep set is every entry the invocation itself wrote (`Env::hold_trashed`), plus, newest first, every entry within `KENDEX_TRASH_KEEP_DAYS` days (default `DEFAULT_KEEP_DAYS`) while the running total of bytes stays within `KENDEX_TRASH_KEEP_MB` MB (default `DEFAULT_KEEP_MB`); the entry that crosses the size bound goes, and everything older goes unmeasured. Enforced by `crates/core/src/trash/tests.rs::entries_past_the_age_bound_go_and_the_rest_stay`, `::entries_past_the_size_bound_go_oldest_first`, `::an_entry_this_invocation_wrote_survives_its_pass` and `crates/cli/tests/trash_cli.rs::an_apply_brings_the_trash_within_its_bounds_and_keeps_what_it_wrote`.
2. The pass fails closed: a bound that is not a count, a trash that will not read, or an entry that will not measure or remove stops it with what is left intact and the count of what went before, and the verb prints the reason as a warning rather than failing; a bound exported empty or as whitespace reads as unset. Enforced by `crates/core/src/trash/tests.rs::a_setting_that_is_not_a_count_stops_the_pass_with_everything_intact`, `::a_setting_exported_empty_reads_as_the_default`, `::a_removal_that_fails_stops_the_pass_and_reports_what_went_before`, `::an_entry_that_will_not_measure_stops_the_pass_and_the_listing`, `::a_trash_that_will_not_read_stops_the_pass` and `crates/cli/tests/trash_cli.rs::a_bound_that_is_not_a_count_removes_nothing_and_an_empty_one_is_the_default`.
3. `kendex trash empty` with no `--older-than`, or with an age of zero, asks first, and with no terminal refuses naming `--yes`, before it removes anything; with an age it removes only the entries past it, oldest first, so a removal that stops leaves the newest entries and the verb exits nonzero naming the count and the cause. Neither takes what the invocation wrote. Enforced by `crates/cli/tests/trash_cli.rs::empty_without_a_bound_asks_and_refuses_with_nobody_to_ask`, `::empty_older_than_removes_only_the_entries_past_that_age`, `::an_empty_that_stops_exits_nonzero_naming_what_went`, `crates/core/src/trash/tests.rs::empty_takes_every_entry_or_every_entry_past_an_age` and `::an_empty_that_stops_leaves_the_newest_entries`.

## Decisions

- Two bounds, age and size, measured by one walk of the kept entries per pass and no size record beside each entry; mirrors in the source cache stay unbounded: [D004](../decisions/D004-trash-retention.md).
