# D037: An overseer's succession with no measured room takes one account nothing measured

[← Decision Index](INDEX.md)

**Date**: 2026-11-22

**Status**: Active

**Research**: [KEN-5435](https://linear.app/vanillagreen/issue/KEN-5435); the owner's decision is the item's comment of 2026-11-22 (owner Slack 1795367188.622989)

**Supersedes**: [D019](D019-copilot-overseer-succession.md)'s exception for an unmeasured account, which kept only the caller's own account at the context mark; the rest of D019 stands.

**Decision**: An unmeasured account is never room. At any overseer mark, and in the watch's walk for a walled overseer, a succession walk whose entries found no account with room takes one account nothing measured, named `kept_unmeasured` in the walk's answer, with `scope: unmeasured` on its record, its successor and the controller record the transfer writes. That account is the caller's own where the mark leaves it in (a context or memory mark, any harness), on any unmeasured reading unless its successor's sign-in reads signed out. Else it is an account whose usage query was refused at the usage endpoint with HTTP 429 and kept no figure, and whose successor's sign-in reads held. Measured room elsewhere wins. As that fallback, a 429 from the token endpoint, a 401 or 403, an unreachable endpoint, a reading with no data and a signed-out account are never taken. At a headroom, rate or wall mark the caller's own account stays excluded, and an unreadable judge keeps nothing.

**Why**: The owner ruled that the walk takes this account. It replaces a wall that is otherwise certain. A 429 from the usage endpoint shows the token was accepted and the plan answered; the token endpoint's 429 posted no usage query, the other refusals show neither, and a 401 or 403 with an unread profile is how an ended plan reads. The caller's own account needs no such answer: the session running on it is the proof the account serves, whatever its usage query said. If the account is in fact at its limit, the successor stops at its first turn and a person moves it by hand, as without the rule. A failed attempt leaves the predecessor in control. A readiness failure holds its account out of every succession walk, the watch's included, for a bounded time; a refusal the owner repairs on the account, a sign-in before the window opened or a first-run step, holds nothing. orch's oversee.md § Succession step 6 names the notes that hold.

**Rejected**: D019's rule alone: on 2026-11-21 and 22 every overseer walk answered `none` while the main Claude account had a live plan, and each successor was started by hand. Every unmeasured status as the fallback: it would take an ended plan's 401.

**Revisit when**: The listing can measure an account its usage query refused with a 429, by a kept figure or a reading taken with its token, or a successor taken this way walls inside its first turn.
