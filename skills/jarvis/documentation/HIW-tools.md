# How does Jarvis use my computer and accounts?

Jarvis uses the tools available for the work you authorize and checks the result at the service or application. It keeps automation browsing separate from your browsing session. Credentials and official originals remain in their protected stores.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    You[Your permitted action] -->|bounds| Jarvis[Jarvis]
    Jarvis -->|uses| Tool[Task browser or supported tool]
    Tool -->|checks at| Service[Intended account and service]
```

## The action

Your instructions define which actions Jarvis can take. Its defaults allow reversible, free delegated work. Deletion, irreversible changes, spending and external commitments need approval for the specific action unless your standing grant covers it. A send in your name needs approval of the exact text and recipient.

Before using your mouse or keyboard, Jarvis describes the action and waits for agreement. Work uses a separate empty target so it does not type into your draft.

## The browser and account

Automation uses an assistant-owned browser profile. Jarvis checks the site, account and destination before entering values, reads them back without exposing protected fields, and checks the result after submission. A form filled in is not yet a successful submission.

A sign-in that needs your phone remains your step. A persistent bot check is handed to you rather than bypassed. A task's tabs close when the work ends.

## Credentials and originals

Credentials move through the password manager or another supported protected interface. Temporary transfer files are private and removed when the receiving operation ends. Secret values stay out of conversation, recovery notes, logs and captures.

Official originals remain unchanged in your private record store, apart from working copies. Filing, altering or sending one needs explicit authorization. Memory holds metadata rather than the original's contents or protected identifiers.

## Recovery and your choices

An uncertain send remains uncertain until its result is checked. Recovery starts by reading the failure. Replacing important data follows checked preservation and the relevant approval boundary.

Your Jarvis home selects Jarvis's profiles, account references, permissions and retention policy. A connected account supplies access; it does not enlarge the task's authority.
