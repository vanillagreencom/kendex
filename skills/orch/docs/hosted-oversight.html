Hosted oversight

How one overseer supervises lanes that run on another machine: what runs where, which credential each part spends, how mail moves, and when a lane or the overseer hands off. The rules: oversee.md and oversee-events.md. The machines themselves: the fleet repository's docs/html/remote-fleet.html.

The one idea

  • An overseer follows one watch. The watch reads files and panes, and types into a lane only the one line that wakes it to read unread mail.
  • A lane talks to its overseer through a mailbox, two files under its worktree. Nothing else carries the lane's words out; the overseer also reads the lane's pane, and pastes into it only as oversee-lanes.md § Talking to a lane allows.
  • On a hosted fleet the lane's worktree and CLI live in a sandbox. The overseer's window on the control VM runs the lane's harness in it over SSH, so the pane reads the same as a local lane.
  • The watch reads a hosted lane's mailbox, harness status, workflow state and status file through its provider's verbs. The overseer never reads a lane's transcript.
  • A hosted lane waiting on a merge can be parked: its harness and its sandbox stop with its disk kept, and a relaunch starts it again. When a lane is parked and what ends a park: oversee-lanes.md § Parking a merge wait.
  • A lane hands off at its marks, and the item continues in a fresh session. The overseer succeeds itself the same way. The marks and who judges each: Handoff and succession, below.

What runs where

The control checkout receives the reviewed, committed outputs through sync-base after they merge; who runs refresh and verification on a hosted fleet: oversee-events.md § merged and SKILL.md, Item work stays in lanes.

Credentials

Three kinds of credential exist. The two logins rotate; the lane token does not. A part that spends the wrong kind, or measures through a copy that another machine also renews, fails in ways the account itself does not.

Mail

A mailbox is two append-only files under a worktree's tmp/lane-mail/<item>/: to-overseer.jsonl and to-lane.jsonl, each line one message with an id, a kind and a sender. The overseer's own inbox is the overseer item in its checkout. How a write appends and a read moves its cursor: the mailbox package's lane-mail --help. A hosted mailbox is appended through the provider's append verb, and the watch reads it at each mail turn through lane-mail drain --host, which fetches each file with the provider's cat.

Handoff and succession

Work moves to a fresh session at a mark, from a record on disk that is the whole state the successor needs. The context mark: the shared context rule; the record's fields: Hand off a lane; the launch words that take automatic compaction away where a harness has them: Compaction.

The lane fires its own trigger: the lane-mail-check turn-end hook reads tokens used and effective capacity through the harness adapter and records that reading in the lane's mailbox. Which marks the hook judges and holds the turn end at, and the hosted lane's account mark it leaves to the overseer: Round Closure. The same hook holds the overseer's own turn end at its marks (Judgement rules, the overseer's own case).
