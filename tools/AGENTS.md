# tools/

This repository's own scripts and the exclusion lists its commit guards read. Nothing here is catalog content: these run in this repository's commit chain and in CI, never on a consumer's machine.

- A script's suite belongs at `tests/<n>.test.sh`, where the `guards-tools` shard of `../.github/workflows/skill-tests.yml` runs it; `release-channel-point` is the one script with none, exercised from [`../crates/cli/tests/release_workflow/`](../crates/cli/tests/release_workflow/) instead. `tests/orch-shard-partition.test.sh` belongs to no script in this directory: it reads [`../.github/workflows/skill-tests.yml`](../.github/workflows/skill-tests.yml) and judges every seam that sends a suite to one shard — the orch name filters, the shell shards' file rosters, the cargo legs' `--test` target rosters and their doc-test request, and the shard names tracked prose cites — and must run outside the orch battery whose suites those filters partition. `ci-job-set` and `ci-aggregate` share one suite, `tests/ci-class-job-set.test.sh`, which reads that workflow the same way, for the contract the two scripts and it hold together. `tests/change-class-action.test.sh` belongs to no script here either: it is the suite of `../.github/actions/change-class/classify`.
- `ci-job-set` turns the change class into one `<lane>=true|false` line per gated lane of that workflow, and `ci-aggregate` holds each required context open over the lanes a class stood down. Neither decides a class: `.github/actions/change-class` reads it once from the shipped classifier and publishes it.
- An exclusion list is `<n>-excludes`, read by the one commit-guards lane named in its own header; the row format and what may be excluded are [`../skills/commit-guards/SKILL.md`](../skills/commit-guards/SKILL.md).
- `guard` and `setup` are described in the root [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) § Commands; `guard` is where a repo-specific rule is added, one lane per rule.
- `lane-setup` is the executable body behind [`../.fleet-setup`](../.fleet-setup). It installs clone dependencies before the fleet creates a worktree; its suite is `tests/lane-setup.test.sh`.
- `bash32-lint` and `bash32-parse` hold this directory and the shell the catalog ships to Bash 3.2; where each runs is [`../skills/AGENTS.md`](../skills/AGENTS.md).
- `release-digests` and `release-channel-point` belong to the release feed, which [`../docs/architecture/updates.md`](../docs/architecture/updates.md) covers.
- `check-aur-sync` and `publish-aur` are the AUR side of [`../packaging/README.md`](../packaging/README.md) § Publishing: the first holds each `packaging/arch/<name>/` PKGBUILD to its `.SRCINFO` (and, with `--remote`, to the AUR), the second pushes them from `.github/workflows/publish-aur.yml`, and its `--check-key` proof of the AUR key has its own suite, `tests/publish-aur-check-key.test.sh`. `check-aur-sync` is Python, the one script here that is; its suite is beside the others. `publish-homebrew` is the tap side: it copies `packaging/homebrew/` into `vanillagreencom/homebrew-kendex` from `.github/workflows/publish-homebrew.yml`, deferring a recipe that still pins a placeholder, and its suite holds the must-fail control for that guard.
- `harness-smoke` answers whether each harness on the machine loads what kendex installed for it, and what reaches a lane when a directive is sent to its mailbox, on every harness the `lane-mail-check` row of `../hooks/README.md` gives a delivery mechanism: through that hook's event where the harness runs it, and through the harness's own listing of the wait-point instruction where it runs no hooks; it runs installed CLIs against scratch projects and writes nothing to the repository.
